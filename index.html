LatentGAT

Brain tumour segmentation with graph attention at the U-Net bottleneck

A 3D U-Net whose bottleneck is treated as a graph: each latent node attends to its 5 nearest neighbours across 4 heads, so distant tumour sub-regions share context before decoding. Trained on BraTS2021 (1,251 cases), then tested on three cohorts it had never seen.

HIGGS: logistic regression vs neural network

How much does model capacity matter at 11 million samples?

Both models were trained on 16 slices of the HIGGS collision dataset (11M events, 28 features), from 1% to 100%, to find where extra data stops paying off. The network's accuracy is already 76.6% at 30% of the data and only reaches 76.8% at 100%.

Explainable AI · Medical imaging

Multi-Objective Selection of Hybrid Deep–Radiomic Features for Brain Tumor MRI Classification with Transparent Fuzzy Rule-Based Inference

Muhammad Azeem Bhatti, Khadija Tul Kubra, Umme Zahoora, Tanja Pavleska, Asifullah Khan

A three-objective search (an opposition-based Harris Hawks extension of NSGA-II) narrows 2,420 hybrid deep and radiomic features to 25, balancing F1-score, feature count and redundancy. A neuro-fuzzy classifier (ANFIS) then explains each MRI decision with readable if–then rules.

Federated learning · Medical imaging

A Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning Framework with Variance-Based Aggregation and Customized U-Net for Brain Tumor Segmentation

Sumer Iqbal, Khadija Tul Kubra, Muhammad Azeem Bhatti, Farquleet Farhat Gondal, Saddam Hussain Khan, Umme Zahoora

Trains a lightweight attention U-Net across hospitals without moving any scans. A new aggregation method, Fed-PLM, merges each site's update along its direction of greatest variation; on BraTS 2021 the federated model comes within 0.01 Dice of centralised training.
